# EvaraOS — unified business operations vision
Date: 2026-10-09
Status: Creative and product planning only. No production changes.

EvaraOS's direction is to combine sales territories, lead management, role-specific staff tools, scheduling, dispatch, finance views and a conversational EvaraOS AI assistant in a single experience.

Verified source foundations:
- Territory and lead maps: public/territory-map.html, public/assets/js/field-ops-realtime.js, public/assets/js/offline-leads.js
- Dispatch and voluntary workforce location sharing: public/assets/js/dispatch-map.js
- Roles and company-scoped access: public/assets/js/access-control.js
- Payroll summaries: public/payroll.html, public/assets/js/payroll.js
- AI chat: public/ai.html, public/assets/js/ai-chat.js
- Marketplace lifecycle: docs/MARKETPLACE_PROGRESS.md

Product concepts pending validation:
- Field-sales map with prospect pins, territory assignment, visit status, follow-up and conversion stages.
- Explicit shift start/end with privacy-aware, work-hours-only field presence and manager/team visibility.
- Self-serve access to eligible earnings between regular payout cycles. Payroll, contractor settlement and earned-wage access need different accounting and regulatory controls; not verified as operational.
- Organizational hierarchy from company to branch/team with backend-enforced scoped permissions.
- Contextual EvaraOS AI for authorized work summaries, next-step suggestions and drafting.

Marketing rule: distinguish implemented code, deployed and tested features, and future plans. Illustrative imagery must not be represented as real staff, offices, customer data or released product screens. The customer-facing marketing collection is a later, separate phase. Retain the original official EvaraOS red E; no name/logo change.
